Setup Instructions

🎵 Audio Analyzer

↑ Drag this to your bookmarks bar

  • Drag the button above to your browser's bookmarks bar
  • Go to YouTube, Spotify, or any page with audio
  • Play the audio/video first
  • Click the bookmarklet from your bookmarks
  • The overlay will appear on the page - click "Start Capture"!
💡 Tip: Make sure audio is playing before you click "Start Capture". The analyzer detects audio/video elements on the page automatically.
