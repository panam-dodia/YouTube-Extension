Audio Analyzer Overlay

Capture and analyze audio from any webpage with a floating overlay

Method 1: Bookmarklet (Easiest)

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any webpage with audio to activate the analyzer overlay ON THAT PAGE:
🎵 Audio Analyzer
  • Drag the "🎵 Audio Analyzer" button above to your browser's bookmarks bar
  • Go to YouTube, Spotify, or any webpage with audio/video
  • Play the audio/video first
  • Click the bookmarklet - the overlay will appear on that same page!
  • Click "Start Capture" to analyze the audio playing on the page
Note: The bookmarklet works by injecting the audio analyzer directly into the current page, so you can see visualizations while the audio plays!

Method 2: Direct Launch

Click this button to open a standalone page with the audio analyzer, then select which tab to capture:
Launch Audio Analyzer

Features

  • Draggable Overlay: Move the analyzer anywhere on the screen
  • Collapsible: Minimize when not in use
  • Real-time Waveform: See audio amplitude over time
  • Frequency Spectrum: Visual representation of audio frequencies
  • Volume Meter: Dynamic volume level indicator
  • Audio Analysis: Peak frequency, average volume, and more
