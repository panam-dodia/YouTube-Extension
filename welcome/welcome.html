Welcome to TalkBridge!

Thank you for installing TalkBridge. Break language barriers and understand any YouTube video with real-time AI translation, voice dubbing, and intelligent Q&A.

What You Can Do

Real-Time Translation

Translate YouTube videos into your preferred language with synchronized audio dubbing powered by advanced AI.

Full Transcripts

Access complete video transcripts in the original language for easy reference and better understanding.

AI Q&A Assistant

Ask questions about the video content or anything beyond and get intelligent answers in your language.

Getting Started

1

Open YouTube

Navigate to any YouTube video you want to translate or understand better

2

Click the TalkBridge Button

Look for the floating TalkBridge button on the bottom right corner of the video page

3

Configure Settings

Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar to select your preferred language and enable features

4

Enjoy!

Start watching with real-time translation, ask questions anytime, and explore content in your language
